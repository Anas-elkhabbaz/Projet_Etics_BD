“The so-called ‘booking questions exemption’ exempts ‘from Miranda’s coverage

The Court concludes that the custodial questioning after the agents reentered the

11

room and Defendant unequivocally requested an attorney does not fall within the “routine

12

booking question exception.” Pennsylvania v. Muniz, 496 U.S. at 601. This exception to

13

Miranda does not include questioning that “the police should know are reasonably likely

14

to elicit an incriminating response from the suspect. The later portion of this definition

15

focuses primarily upon the perceptions of the suspect, rather than the intent of the police.”

16

Rhode Island v. Innis, 446 U.S. 291, 301 (1980). “‘Any knowledge the police may have

17

had concerning the unusual susceptibility of a defendant to a particular form of persuasion

18

might be an important factor in determining’ what the police reasonably should have

19

known.” Pennsylvania v. Muniz, 496 U.S. at 611 quoting Rhode Island v. Innis, 446 U.S.

20

at 302.

22

including her full name, date of birth, address, phone number, email address, location of

23

her birth, her current residence, height, scars, tattoos, type of employment, place of
